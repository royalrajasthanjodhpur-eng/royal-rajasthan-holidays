OFFICIAL LOGO
=============

Place the official Royal Rajasthan Holidays logo file in this folder, named exactly:

    logo.png

Requirements
------------
* High-resolution PNG with a transparent or white background
* Recommended height: at least 400px (the site renders it at 40–46px, retina-safe)
* Do NOT recolour, crop, redraw or stretch the mark — the site never applies filters to it
* Clear space is preserved automatically by the Logo component (padding + white chip
  when the header sits over a dark hero image)

Until logo.png is present, a temporary typographic lockup renders in its place.
It is replaced automatically the moment the official file is added.
